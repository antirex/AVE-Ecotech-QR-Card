07 / FAQ

Questions we get on site.

Something else? Call +91 83294 34631.

Where can I use it?

Footpaths, parking and driveways, terraces and podiums, gardens, pool decks, and industrial or lab floors.

What does a tile weigh?

2.5 kg per tile. Each tile covers 1 sq.ft and is 25 mm thick — about 40% lighter than a cement paver.

Does it break?

It is designed to handle 20 tonnes and does not spall like cement. Ask for a sample and test it yourself.

Can it handle my 20-tonne truck?

Yes. EcoTiles are designed to handle 20 tonnes on a properly prepared base.

Is it normal cement packing or special packing?

EcoTiles are hand-laid by a regular paving crew, with minimal water curing and no cutting. Tell us your base and we will share the laying guide.

Which colours can I have?

Five standard colours: green, charcoal, terracotta, slate blue and sand. Custom colours are available on bulk orders.

Is it algae proof?

Yes. Tests show 0% algae formation, and the tile absorbs under 1% water. Reports on request.
