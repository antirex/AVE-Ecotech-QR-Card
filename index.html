About Us

Sustainable materials for a circular economy

AVE EcoTech converts plastic and industrial waste into high-performance and environmentally responsible construction materials.

Using waste plastic and discarded foundry silica dust, we engineer durable, lightweight products that pave the way for a circular economy.

Aligned with India's Smart City and Net Zero goals, our solutions are sustainable, cost-effective and certification-ready.

Delivering scalable, sustainable infrastructure for a low-carbon future.
